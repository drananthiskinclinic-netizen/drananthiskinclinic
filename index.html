About Dr. Ananthi

Dr. M. Ananthi is a consultant dermatologist and senior skin specialist with more than 16 years of experience.

She leads the Department of Dermatology at Villupuram Medical College as Associate Professor and HOD, and sees patients at her clinics in Panruti and Cuddalore. She has made 10+ scientific presentations at national conferences in dermatology, venereology and leprosy.
